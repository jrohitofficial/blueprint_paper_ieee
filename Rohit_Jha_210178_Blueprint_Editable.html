3. Master Consolidated Research Gap Contribution Matrix

Key Research Dimension State of the Art in Base Papers Critical Unresolved Research Gap How My Project Fulfills the Gap
Geographic Scope & Scalability Neupane et al. models Karnali river basin; Maharjan et al. limits to Mohana-Khutiya. Isolated river basin studies fail to capture macro-level hydrometeorological patterns required for a unified, national-level strategy. Expands scope to a National-Scale analysis covering the entire country, integrating diverse ecological zones.
Spatial Validation Strategy (Leakage Paradox) Thapa et al. and Neupane et al. rely on standard random data splitting (e.g., 70/30). Standard splits suffer from geographic data leakage (adjacent correlated pixels bleed into the test set), inflating accuracy. Implements Spatially-Robust Validation (GroupKFold) by districts to strictly separate regions during training, revealing true generalizability.
Predictor Dynamism Base papers rely on static environmental factors (Elevation, LULC) or basic averages. Static maps predict where floods occur theoretically, but fail to predict when they trigger based on changing weather. Integrates dynamic triggers, specifically antecedent rainfall accumulation and real-time soil moisture alongside BIPAD records.
Model Interpretability (XAI) Base papers output standard nominal accuracy and basic tree-based "Feature Importance". Feature importance shows which variables matter but fails to explain how they interact, keeping the model a "black box". Employs advanced Explainable AI (SHAP) for dependence plots, proving exactly how variables interact to trigger floods.
